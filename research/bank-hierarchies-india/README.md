# Bank Career Hierarchies — End-to-End Levels & Bangalore Salary Benchmarks

**Scope:** every major US, European, UK, Japanese and Indian bank / financial-services GCC that
employs people in India, with the **complete** title ladder (no skipped rungs, including the junior
levels most write-ups omit) and expected **Bengaluru** compensation.

**As of:** August 2026 · **Currency:** ₹ (INR) · **LPA** = lakhs per annum · **Cr** = crore (100 lakh)

---

## 0. How to read this document

### 0.1 What the salary numbers mean

Unless stated otherwise, every rupee figure is **total annual CTC = fixed base + target/typical
annual bonus**, for **Bengaluru**, for a **lateral hire in 2026**.

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Fixed CTC | Base + allowances + employer PF + gratuity. What you see on the offer letter's "fixed" line. |
| Variable / bonus | Annual performance bonus. 8–15% of fixed at junior levels, 15–30% at VP, 30–100%+ at MD/front-office. |
| Total CTC | Fixed + target bonus. **This is what the tables show.** |
| RSU / deferred | Stock or deferred cash. Meaningful only from AVP/VP upward, and mostly at US banks. Called out where it materially changes the number. |

### 0.2 The ±35% rule

The **same level at the same bank in Bangalore** can differ by 35% or more depending on:

1. **Function** — Technology / Quant / Data Science pay **20–45% above** the same grade in
   Operations, KYC, Reconciliations or Fund Accounting. Risk, Finance, Audit and Model Validation
   sit in between.
2. **Internal promo vs. external lateral** — an external lateral is typically **20–35% richer**
   at the same grade. Internally promoted AVPs frequently earn less than newly hired Officers.
3. **Legal entity** — the captive/GCC entity (e.g. BA Continuum, Wells Fargo India Solutions,
   State Street Syntel Services) pays less than the licensed bank branch entity for the same title.
4. **Year of joining** — 2021–22 hires carry a permanent premium from the post-COVID talent war.
5. **Bengaluru vs. Mumbai vs. Pune/Chennai/Hyderabad** — Bengaluru is generally the **highest-paying
   Indian location for technology**; Mumbai is highest for front-office markets/IB roles.

### 0.3 Confidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Published or overwhelmingly corroborated (official pay scales, job postings, hundreds of data points). |
| **B** | Consistent across multiple independent employee-reported sources. Standard market understanding. |
| **C** | Reported but variable across business units / contested between sources. **Verify with the recruiter.** |

Grade **codes** (C12, GCB5, BA4, E2, M6, letter grades) are almost never officially published by
banks. Where a code is given below it is the widely-used internal convention — treat codes as **C**
even where the *title order* is **A** or **B**.

## 1. The universal rung scale

Titles are not comparable across banks. Use this normalised scale as the pivot.

| Rung | What it actually is | Typical exp. | Bengaluru total CTC (blended) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R0** | Trainee / apprentice / off-roll / non-officer support | 0–1 | ₹2.5–5 L |
| **R1** | Junior analyst / processing associate (entry, on-roll) | 0–2 | ₹4–9 L |
| **R2** | Analyst — owns a process end-to-end | 2–4 | ₹8–16 L |
| **R3** | Senior analyst / senior associate — reviewer, SME | 4–7 | ₹14–26 L |
| **R4** | Lead / officer / team leader — **first supervisory rung** | 6–10 | ₹20–35 L |
| **R5** | **AVP-equivalent** — manages a team or is a senior IC | 8–13 | ₹25–48 L |
| **R6** | **VP-equivalent** — manages managers / principal IC | 12–18 | ₹40–90 L |
| **R7** | SVP / Director-equivalent — functional head in India | 16–22 | ₹80 L–1.6 Cr |
| **R8** | ED / Senior Director — sub-business head | 20–26 | ₹1.3–2.5 Cr |
| **R9** | **MD** — India site / global function head | 24+ | ₹2–5 Cr |
| **R10** | Group MD / EVP / country CEO | 28+ | ₹5 Cr+ |

> **Warning:** *R6 "VP-equivalent" is a band, not a point.* At Goldman a VP is R6. At Wells Fargo the
> officer title "VP" attaches at R4–R5. At UBS the equivalent title is "Director". Never negotiate on
> the word "VP" alone — negotiate on rung, scope and money.

## 3. Detailed ladders + Bengaluru salary bands

### 3.1 State Street  ·  Confidence: title order **A/B**, grade codes **C**

**Bengaluru footprint:** Very large (Manyata / EGL / Global Delivery). Also Hyderabad, Chennai,
Coimbatore, Pune, Mumbai. Entities include State Street Corporation India, State Street Syntel
Services and Charles River (a State Street company, Bengaluru).

**Complete ladder — nothing omitted:**

| # | Level | Reported grade band | Typical India job titles | Exp. (yrs) | **Bengaluru total CTC** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Apprentice / Intern | — | Apprentice, Trainee | 0 | ₹2.4–4.5 L |
| 2 | **Associate 1** | ~200–260 | Associate, Ops Associate, Analyst | 0–2 | ₹4–7.5 L |
| 3 | **Associate 2** | ~280–300 | Associate 2, Senior Ops Associate | 1.5–4 | ₹6–10 L |
| 4 | **Senior Associate** | ~300–340 | Senior Associate, Specialist, SME | 3–7 | ₹9–16 L |
| 5 | **Officer** *(first FLSA-exempt / officer rung)* | ~360–420 | Officer, Manager, Senior Manager, Lead | 6–11 | ₹15–26 L |
| 6 | **Assistant Vice President** *(commonly split AVP-1 / AVP-2 in India)* | ~500–520 | AVP, AVP–Technology, AVP–Fund Accounting | 9–15 | **₹23–42 L** (median ≈ ₹31 L) |
| 7 | **Vice President** | ~520–600 | VP, VP–Technology, Head of a function | 13–20 | ₹42–75 L |
| 8 | **Managing Director** ⚠️ *(above VP)* | ~600+ | MD, MD–Global Delivery | 18–25 | ₹80 L–1.4 Cr |
| 9 | **Senior Vice President** ⚠️ *(above MD)* | — | SVP, Head of India Ops/Tech | 22+ | ₹1.3–2.5 Cr |
| 10 | **Executive Vice President** | — | EVP (globally; effectively none based in India) | 26+ | ₹3 Cr+ |
| 11 | Vice Chairman / President / CEO | — | Global executive committee | — | — |

**Facts that trip people up at State Street:**
- Sequence is **Associate 1 → Associate 2 → Senior Associate → Officer → AVP → VP → MD → SVP → EVP**.
- There is **no Executive Director** title.
- **Officer** is the pivot: it is where you cross into exempt/officer status. An "Officer" doing
  people management is often titled "Manager" or "Senior Manager" on LinkedIn — same grade.
- AVP is frequently a **two-step band** (AVP-1 → AVP-2) with no title change, only grade and money.
  This is a common cause of "I was promoted but the title didn't change".
- Glassdoor Bengaluru data (Aug 2026, ~1,500 submissions) spans **₹3 L (apprentice) to ₹1.04 Cr (MD)** —
  consistent with the table above.

## 4. The traps — every place these ladders break

This is the section to re-read before you accept an offer or build a comparison chart.

| # | Trap | Where it bites |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **MD is not always near the top.** | **State Street**: AVP → VP → **MD** → **SVP** → EVP. A State Street MD ≈ JPM Executive Director ≈ Citi SVP. State Street's SVP is the one comparable to a bulge-bracket MD. |
| 2 | **"Director" often means VP.** | **UBS**, **Standard Chartered** (Grade 5), **RBC**, **Amex** (Band 40). At all four, Director sits well below the US-bank Director/MD line. |
| 3 | **Director below VP.** | **Fidelity** — Director → VP → SVP. The reverse of BlackRock. |
| 4 | **The invisible middle rung.** | **Northern Trust: Second Vice President** between Officer and VP. **HDFC: Deputy Vice President** between AVP and VP. **BNY: VP-1/VP-2**. **State Street: AVP-1/AVP-2**. These are the rungs comparison charts drop. |
| 5 | **Bands that count downwards.** | **HSBC** (GCB 8 junior → 0 senior), **Standard Chartered** (Grade 8 → 1), **Société Générale** (Band 9 → 3). "GCB 4" is more senior than "GCB 5". |
| 6 | **Sub-steps hidden inside one grade.** | SG **8B → 8A** (B is lower). HSBC tech **6.2 → 6.1** and **5.2 → 5.1** (higher decimal = more junior). ICICI **DM-I → DM-II**, **M-I → M-II**, **CM-I → CM-II**. |
| 7 | **Title on the offer ≠ internal grade.** | **Wells Fargo** (functional title *Lead / Senior Lead / Principal / Associate Manager* vs. hidden officer title VP/ED/MD). **HSBC** (Consultant Specialist vs. AVP, same GCB 5). **Barclays** (BA4 can out-earn AVP). |
| 8 | **The same code means different things by division.** | **JPMorganChase** 601/602 map differently in Banking vs. Technology. **Citi** C11 is AVP in some Ops units, Manager/Sr. Analyst in most Tech units. |
| 9 | **Titles that don't exist.** | JPMorganChase has **no AVP**. Goldman has **no AVP, SVP or ED** (in India). Morgan Stanley has **no AVP or SVP**. State Street has **no ED**. If a recruiter quotes one of these, they're translating, not stating a grade. |
| 10 | **Entry rung is not "Analyst" everywhere.** | State Street starts at **Associate 1** (Analyst isn't the entry title). Deutsche starts at **NCT** below Analyst. BofA India starts at **Team Developer**. Citi starts in the **C01–C08 non-officer** range. Barclays starts at **BA1**. |
| 11 | **Captive entity vs. bank branch.** | Same title, different legal employer (BA Continuum vs. Bank of America NA; State Street Syntel Services vs. State Street Corporation; DBOI vs. Deutsche Bank AG) — different pay grid, different benefits, different mobility. |
| 12 | **PSU "scale" ≠ private "grade".** | A PSU **Scale V / AGM** manages a whole zone but earns roughly what a private-bank **AVP** earns in Bengaluru. Comparing PSU and private on CTC alone is misleading — perquisites and pension close much of the gap. |

## 6. Practical guidance

**When you get an offer, always ask for these four things in writing:**
1. The **internal grade code** (C12 / GCB5 / BA4 / P4 / M6 / D2 / Grade 4 / job level number) —
   not just the title.
2. The **officer title** attached to that grade, if the bank separates them (Wells Fargo, HSBC).
3. **Fixed CTC, target bonus %, and whether RSUs/deferred cash apply** at that grade — deferral
   kicks in around AVP/VP at most US and European banks and can be 10–40% of your bonus.
4. **How many steps exist inside your grade** before the next title change (AVP-1 → AVP-2,
   VP-1 → VP-2, 8B → 8A, DM-I → DM-II). This tells you the real promotion clock.

**Typical time-in-grade in India (median, good performer):** R1→R2 ≈ 2 yrs · R2→R3 ≈ 2–3 yrs ·
R3→R4 ≈ 2–3 yrs · R4→R5 (AVP) ≈ 3–4 yrs · R5→R6 (VP) ≈ **4–6 yrs** (the biggest bottleneck) ·
R6→R7 ≈ 5–7 yrs · R7→R9 (MD) — a minority ever get there.

**The AVP→VP wall is where most banking careers in Bengaluru plateau.** The reliable ways through
it are: (a) switch banks laterally at the AVP→VP boundary — externals get the title far more easily
than internals; (b) move from Operations into Technology, Risk or Product; (c) take a global role
rather than a "captive delivery" role, since captive-only ladders usually cap around VP/SVP.

## 7. Confidence, limitations & sources

### What is solid
- Title **order** for State Street, Goldman, JPMorganChase, Morgan Stanley, Deutsche Bank, UBS,
  Northern Trust, Nomura and Citi.
- PSU bank pay scales — these are **published** (IBA 12th Bipartite Settlement).

### What is softer
- **All grade code mappings** (C-grades, GCB numbers, BA numbers, letter grades, M-numbers, E/D
  grades, P/M levels). Banks do not publish these; the mappings above are the widely-used
  convention, corroborated across employee-reported sources, and they **do vary by business unit**.
- **Every salary figure.** These are synthesised from crowdsourced platforms (Glassdoor, levels.fyi,
  6figr, AmbitionBox, Fishbowl, Blind, Grapevine) plus published India-market ranges. Crowdsourced
  data over-represents technology and under-represents operations, so treat the ranges as
  *negotiation reference points*, not quotes.
- Bank of America India (BA Continuum) operational titles, BNP Paribas ISPL and SG GSC band
  structures — reported, not confirmed.
