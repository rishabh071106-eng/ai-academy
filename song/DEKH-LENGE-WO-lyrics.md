# DEKH LENGE WO
### (They'll See)

**Genre:** Hindi / Hinglish Hip-Hop — dark motivational trap-boombap
**BPM:** 88 (hats ride 16ths, so the flow *feels* like 176)
**Key:** C# minor
**Mood:** grimy, hungry, rising
**Total length:** ~4:11

## PERFORMANCE / RECORDING NOTES

**Flow map**
- Verse 1 → lay back, ~8–10 syllables per bar. You're tired in this verse. Sound tired.
- Verse 2 → ~12–14 syllables per bar, land hard on the bolded word (that's the snare, beat 2 and 4).
- Verse 3 → ~16–18 syllables per bar. Straight 16ths. Rehearse it slow at 70 BPM first, then bring it up to 88.
- Hook → *fewer* words than the verse. That contrast is what makes a hook a hook. Don't over-write it.

**Vocal layers (this is what makes it sound "professionally big")**
1. **Main** — centre, loudest.
2. **Double** — record the exact same take again, pan it dead centre under the main at −6 dB. Instant thickness.
3. **Punch-ins** — only record the **bold** words a third time, pan them hard L and R at −10 dB.
4. **Ad-libs** — the `(...)` bits, pan L/R, add heavy reverb + a low-pass so they sit behind.
5. **Hook stack** — do the hook 4 times: main, double, one an octave up (quiet), one whispered. Blend.

**Mic technique**
- 6 inches from the mic, slightly off-axis so plosives (p, b, kh) don't pop.
- Record the verse in 4-bar chunks. Nobody records 16 bars in one take.
- Leave the first 8 bars (intro) silent in your take — that's where the spoken intro goes, recorded separately, closer to the mic, quieter.
