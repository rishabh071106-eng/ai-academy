# VP at State Street vs VP at JP Morgan — Hierarchy, Seniority & Salary Deep-Dive

**Date:** 2026-07-04
**Method:** Multi-agent deep-research run — 5 parallel search angles → 30+ sources fetched → every factual claim adversarially verified by 3 independent fact-checkers (a claim dies on 2/3 refutations). 49 agents, 294 tool calls. Findings below are tagged **[VERIFIED 3-0]** where all three verifiers confirmed the claim against live sources, and **[Supporting / unverified]** where data came from the search sweep but did not go through (or did not survive) formal verification.

---

## TL;DR — the headline

**"Vice President" does NOT mean the same thing at the two firms.** JP Morgan runs the classic, compressed investment-bank ladder where VP is a genuinely senior middle-management rung (one visible step below Executive Director). State Street runs a custody-bank ladder with *extra* rungs (Officer, AVP) inserted below VP, and hands the VP title out far more broadly — classic **title inflation**.

- A **State Street AVP ≈ JP Morgan Associate / Senior Associate** (India data: SSGA AVP earns ~45–50% of a JPM VP's median package) **[VERIFIED 3-0]**
- A **State Street VP ≈ JP Morgan VP at best — and frequently below it** in both pay and real seniority; it does **not** map to JPM Executive Director.
- On the same platform (Glassdoor US), the average JPM VP out-earns the average State Street VP by roughly **$240K vs $185K** — same title, ~30% pay gap. *[Supporting]*

## 1. The two title ladders, side by side

### JP Morgan Chase — the classic IB ladder **[VERIFIED 3-0]**

JPM treats "Vice President" as a formal, distinct corporate grade with internal level codes, and Levels.fyi models it as a dedicated level with its own per-location salary pages:

| Rung | Internal level code | Typical years of experience* |
|---|---|---|
| Analyst | 502 | 0–2 (entry, bachelor's) |
| Associate | 601 | ~2+ (or master's entry) |
| Senior Associate | 602 | 3–4 |
| **Vice President** | **603** | **~5–6** |
| Executive Director | 604 | 10+ |
| Managing Director | — | ~15 (analyst→MD industry norm) |
| (Vice Chair) | — | above MD |

\* Years per rung from eFinancialCareers, CareerClimb and Blind practitioner threads — *supporting, not formally verified*.

### State Street — the custody-bank ladder *[Supporting — employee-forum sourced]*

Consistent across Quora, Fishbowl and Glassdoor employee threads:

```
Associate 1 → Associate 2 → Senior Associate → Officer → AVP → VP → SVP / MD → EVP
```

Two extra sub-VP rungs (**Officer** and **AVP**) exist that JPM's main ladder simply doesn't have. State Street employees themselves describe VP titles as *"handed out like candy,"* partly to satisfy pay-grade band requirements — direct insider evidence of title inflation. (Forum sources are individually low-reliability; the ladder shape, however, is corroborated across three independent communities and is consistent with the verified pay data below.)

### Why the inflation exists

Industry references (Wall Street Oasis, eFinancialCareers) note that **AVP is predominantly a back/middle-office title** — equivalent to a high-level Associate or low-level VP at a bulge bracket — and that at JPM itself, AVP-style titles appear only in back-office/securities-services areas. State Street is overwhelmingly a custody/asset-servicing (middle/back-office) firm, so its whole ladder is denominated in those inflated units.

## 2. Compensation — United States

| Data point | State Street VP | JP Morgan VP |
|---|---|---|
| Glassdoor avg total pay | **~$185K** (Boston range $150K–$231K; 630 salaries submitted) | **~$242K** |
| Levels.fyi | VP $150K–$259K TC (AVP: $87K–$156K; SVP: $199K–$335K) | Median TC ~$185K–$225K; base median ~$196K ($159K–$242K) + bonus median ~$47K ($35K–$65K); IB VPs avg ~$342K; SWE VP NYC $191K–$293K+ |
| 6figr | $141K–$248K | $170K–**$686K** |

*All US figures are supporting data from the search sweep (crowdsourced aggregators), not formally verified claims.*

**What it highlights:**
- Same nominal title, but JPM's VP distribution sits meaningfully higher and has a **far fatter right tail** ($686K vs $248K top of range on 6figr) — because JPM's VP band includes front-office/IB roles that State Street's custody-heavy VP population lacks.
- State Street's **SVP** band ($199K–$335K) is what actually overlaps JPM's **VP-to-ED** territory — again showing State Street titles run ~1 rung "hot."

- State Street AVP pay (~₹28L) is only **~40% above State Street's firm-wide average** (₹20L) — and Glassdoor alone has 106 AVP submissions — proving AVP is a *widely held mid-level grade*, not a senior title.
- A State Street AVP earns **~45–50% of a JPM Bengaluru VP's median** (₹28–32.5L vs ₹63.1L) → **State Street AVP ≈ JPM Associate/Senior Associate**.
- Even State Street's **VP** (~₹35L, unverified) would sit at only **~55% of JPM's VP median** — i.e., between JPM's Senior Associate and VP bands, nowhere near ED.

## 4. Seniority verdict — what maps to what

| State Street title | Closest JP Morgan equivalent | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Associate 1/2, Senior Associate | Analyst | ladder position |
| Officer | Analyst → Associate boundary | ladder position |
| **AVP** | **Associate / Senior Associate (601–602)** | **[VERIFIED 3-0]** pay ratio + submission volume |
| **VP** | **VP (603) at best; often Senior Associate–VP boundary** | pay data + ladder inference |
| SVP / MD | ED (604) → MD | US band overlap (supporting) |
| EVP | MD / Vice Chair tier | ladder position (supporting) |

**Bottom line on seniority:** a JPM VP is reached at ~5–6 years in a *more selective* ladder and is paid like it; a State Street VP arrives after climbing two extra junior rungs in a *broader* distribution and is paid one notch lighter. **A State Street VP should not present, or be benchmarked, as equivalent to a JPM ED — and in pay terms is often below a JPM VP.** Conversely, moving from a State Street VP seat to a JPM VP seat is a genuine step *up* in market value even though the title looks lateral.

## 5. Refuted claim (killed in adversarial verification)

> ❌ *"State Street 'VP, Technology Manager' in India averages ~₹30L, implying only a ~₹2–3L pay step from AVP to VP."* — **REFUTED 0-3.** The ₹30L figure was a single niche hybrid job-title bucket surfaced on the AVP page, not a representative VP average; broader data (including 6figr's own SS VP page at ~₹35L) contradicts the small-step inference. The *direction* (compressed ladder) may still hold, but this specific quantification did not survive.

## 6. Caveats & open questions

- **All surviving evidence is crowdsourced** (Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, 6figr, forums) — no official HR band documents were obtainable, so no finding is rated above medium confidence despite unanimous verification votes.
- Small samples in places (6figr AVP Technical n=16); ~14% cross-source variance on SS AVP India (₹28L vs ₹32.5L).
- JPM India figures are **software-engineer-specific**; ops/fund-accounting VP comp (where State Street's India workforce concentrates) runs lower at both firms.
- Direct State Street **VP** (not AVP) pay was the weakest link — the only formal claim on it was refuted, so the VP→VP mapping rests partly on inference.
- Not covered by verified claims: typical years-per-rung at State Street, the SVP/EVP tier vs JPM ED/MD in authority terms, and formally verified US-dollar comps.
