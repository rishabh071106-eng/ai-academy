How to Use This Book

Welcome to Whisker Woods Academy — a school in the middle of a forest where the students happen to be animals, and the subject they study happens to be the most exciting idea of our time: artificial intelligence, or AI.

Every chapter is a class. In each one, Professor Hoot the owl teaches one real AI idea, and the animals figure it out by living through a story. You don't need to be good at math. You don't need a computer. You just need to be curious — and you already are, or you wouldn't have opened this book.

To help you get the most out of each class, look out for these signs along the way:

🔍 How It Really Works

This is where we step out of the story and look at the actual algorithm — the real recipe that scientists and engineers use. This is the part that makes you smarter than most grown-ups about AI.

✏️ Try It Yourself

A hands-on activity, puzzle, or experiment you can do with paper, friends, or things around the house. Doing beats reading. Always try these.

💡 Big Idea

The one sentence from the chapter that's worth remembering forever. If you only keep one thing, keep this.

⚠️ Watch Out

A common mistake, a place where AI gets things wrong, or a trap that even real engineers fall into. Knowing these makes you wise.

📖 Word Bank

The grown-up vocabulary for what you just learned — the exact words used by real scientists. Now they're yours, too. Every Word Bank term also lives in The AI Dictionary at the back of the book.

You can read the chapters in order, like a story, or hop straight to one that sounds exciting. Each class stands on its own. But the ideas do build on each other, like blocks in a tower — so if something feels confusing, flipping back a class or two usually helps.
