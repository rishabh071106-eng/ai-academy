The Students

Meet the Class

Eight curious animals, one wise teacher, and a whole forest of ideas to figure out.

Professor Hoot

The wise old owl who runs the Academy. Asks more questions than he answers.

Bolt

A rabbit in a hurry. Wants the fast answer — and learns that fast isn't always smart.

Mango

A monkey who never stops asking “why?” Curious, clever, and a little mischievous.

Pip

A small, careful mouse. Shy, but notices the tiny details everyone else misses.

Tess

A tortoise who is never in a rush. Slow, steady, and almost always right in the end.

Coco

A parrot who repeats what she hears — which makes her perfect for learning about language.

Rex

A loyal dog who loves treats. The best friend in the forest for learning about rewards.

Ziggy

A zebra covered in patterns. Loves sorting, grouping, and spotting what makes things different.
