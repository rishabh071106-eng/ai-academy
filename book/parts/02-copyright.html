This book explains real artificial-intelligence ideas in a simplified, friendly way. The animals, dialogue, and stories are invented to make the ideas easy to picture. The algorithms, however, are real — the same families of methods used by scientists and engineers who build AI today.

The activities in this book are designed to be done with everyday materials and, for anything involving a computer or the internet, with a trusted adult nearby.
