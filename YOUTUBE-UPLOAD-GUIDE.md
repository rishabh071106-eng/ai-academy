# Uploading your space video to YouTube: every setting, start to finish

This is written for a **space video** (black holes, planets, galaxies, a journey through
the universe) uploaded from a **computer** at **studio.youtube.com**. The phone app hides
most of the settings below, including translations, audio tracks and end screens, so use
a laptop for the main upload.

Do the parts in order. Part 1 is one-time channel setup. Parts 2 to 4 are for every video.

---

## First, an honest note on "going viral"

No setting makes a video viral. YouTube shows a video to a small test group, measures two
things, and shows it to more people if both are good:

1. **Click-through rate (CTR):** how many people who *see* the thumbnail click it. This
   comes from the **thumbnail + title**.
2. **Watch time and satisfaction:** how long they stay and whether they like it. This
   comes from the **first 10 seconds** and the pacing.

Everything below does one of three jobs: it helps YouTube understand the video (language,
category, description), it lets the video reach more countries (translations, audio
tracks, subtitles), or it raises CTR and watch time (thumbnail, title, hook, end screen,
Shorts). Spend most of your effort on the thumbnail, the title and the first 10 seconds.

## Part 1: One-time channel setup (15 min)

### 1.1 Verify your phone number. Without this you can't upload a custom thumbnail.
YouTube Studio → **Settings** (bottom left) → **Channel** → **Feature eligibility** →
**Intermediate features** → **Verify phone number**.
This unlocks custom thumbnails, videos longer than 15 minutes, and live streaming.

### 1.2 Fill in the channel
YouTube Studio → **Customization**:

| Setting | What to put |
|---|---|
| Name | Short and space-themed, easy to say, e.g. "Cosmic ___" or "___ Universe" |
| Handle (@name) | Same as the name if it's available |
| Profile picture | 800×800, simple, readable when tiny (a planet, a logo letter) |
| Banner | 2560×1440, keep text inside the centre 1546×423 safe area |
| Description | 2 to 3 lines: what the channel is, how often you post. Put keywords in naturally: "space", "universe", "black holes", "astronomy" |
| Links | Instagram, X, etc. |

### 1.3 Basic info
Studio → **Settings** → **Channel** → **Basic info**:
- **Country of residence:** India (or wherever you live). This is for payments and tax.
  It does **not** limit which countries see your video.
- **Keywords:** `space, universe, black hole, astronomy, cosmos, galaxy, planets, science`

### 1.4 Upload defaults (saves time on every upload)
Studio → **Settings** → **Upload defaults**:
- **Basic info** tab: Visibility **Private** (safer: nothing goes public by accident).
- **Advanced settings** tab: License **Standard YouTube License**, Category
  **Science & Technology**, Comments **On** with **Hold potentially inappropriate comments
  for review**, **Video language** = the language spoken in your video, **Title and
  description language** = the language you write them in.
  If you see **Allow automatic dubbing**, turn it **on** (see Part 4.4).

## Part 2: Prepare these files before you open the upload page

### 2.1 The video file: export settings

| Setting | Use this |
|---|---|
| Container | **MP4** |
| Video codec | **H.264** |
| Resolution | **1920×1080 minimum.** If you can, export at **2560×1440 or 3840×2160 (4K)** |
| Frame rate | Same as your project (24, 30 or 60). Don't convert |
| Bitrate | 1080p: 10–12 Mbps · 1440p: 20–25 Mbps · 4K: 40–50 Mbps |
| Audio | **AAC, 48 kHz, stereo, 320–384 kbps** |

**Why 1440p or 4K matters for space videos in particular:** space videos are mostly dark
gradients, and YouTube's compression turns dark gradients into ugly bands and blocks.
YouTube usually gives better compression to uploads of 1440p and above, so exporting
your 1080p project at 1440p tends to look cleaner on YouTube, even for viewers watching
at 1080p.

**Name the file with its topic**, e.g. `what-happens-inside-a-black-hole.mp4`, not
`final_v3.mp4`.

### 2.2 The first 10 seconds (check before you export)
- Open with your **single most stunning shot**. No logo intro, no "hey guys, welcome
  back", no slow fade from black.
- In the first 5 seconds, say or show **the question the video answers**
  ("What would you see if you fell into a black hole?").
- If YouTube is likely to show this video to people who don't speak your language, keep
  some on-screen text or visuals that make sense even with the sound off.

### 2.3 The thumbnail (this decides your CTR)
- **1280×720** (16:9), JPG or PNG, **under 2 MB**.
- **One** subject: a bright object against black space (a glowing black hole, Earth, a
  planet). Big, centred, high contrast. Push saturation and brightness up, because space
  thumbnails look dull and dark next to other videos.
- **2 to 4 words** of text at most, very thick font, e.g. "INSIDE A BLACK HOLE". The
  words should **add** to the title, not repeat it.
- A human face or an eye reacting raises clicks if it fits your style.
- **Test it small:** shrink it to the size of a matchbox on your phone. If you can't
  tell what it is, simplify.
- Make **2 or 3 versions**. You can A/B test them (Part 3, step 1).
- Don't put a NASA or ESA logo on it, and don't call it "real footage" unless it is.

### 2.4 The title
- **Under 60 characters** so it doesn't get cut off. The YouTube limit is 100.
- Make it a **curiosity question or a big claim** that is still **true**.
- Put the main search words early.
- Examples for space:
  - `What Happens If You Fall Into a Black Hole?`
  - `The Largest Star in the Universe Will Shock You`
  - `A Journey to the Edge of the Universe`
  - `Why Nothing Escapes a Black Hole (Not Even Light)`
- Check what people actually search: YouTube search box autocomplete, and Studio →
  **Analytics** → **Research** tab (if your Studio shows it).

### 2.5 The description (copy this template)

```
[Line 1–2: these show in search results. Say what the video is with the main keywords.]
What really happens when you fall into a black hole? Follow a journey past the event
horizon, through spaghettification, to the singularity, explained simply.

⏱️ Chapters
0:00 Intro
0:45 What is a black hole?
2:10 Crossing the event horizon
3:30 Spaghettification
5:00 The singularity
6:20 What we still don't know

🔭 About this video
[3–5 sentences in plain language. Use the words people search for (black hole, event
horizon, Milky Way, NASA, James Webb...) naturally, without stuffing.]

🎵 Music: [track name, artist, YouTube Audio Library / source]
🖼️ Image credits: [e.g. NASA/JPL-Caltech, ESA/Webb, ...]
🛠️ Made with: [Blender / AI tool / your own code ...]

🔔 Subscribe for a new space video every [day of the week].

#space #blackhole #universe
```

Rules that matter:
- **Chapters:** the first timestamp must be `0:00`, you need at least 3, and each one at
  least 10 seconds long. Chapters show on the progress bar and in Google search.
- **Hashtags:** the first 3 show above your title. Don't use more than about 15. With more
  than 60, YouTube ignores all of them.
- **Credits:** NASA images and video are generally free to use, but credit them and don't
  imply that NASA endorses you. ESA and Hubble/Webb images usually require the credit
  line shown on the image page.

### 2.6 Tags (low importance, 2 minutes)
Tags mostly help with misspellings. Example:
`black hole, blackhole, black holes explained, what is a black hole, space documentary,
universe, astronomy, event horizon, ब्लैक होल, space video, cosmos`

### 2.7 Music: this decides which countries can watch
If you used a **popular song or copyrighted music**, YouTube's Content ID will catch it.
The owner can then take your ad money, **block your video in some countries**, or mute
it. To keep the video watchable everywhere:
- Use **YouTube Audio Library**: Studio → **Audio library** (left menu). Filter
  **Attribution not required**, genres **Ambient** or **Cinematic**. These are safe in
  every country.
- Or use music you made yourself, or properly licensed music.

### 2.8 If your video has a voice-over: extra language audio tracks
See Part 4.3. Prepare one **full-length audio file per language**, the **same length as
the video**, with the same music and sound effects and only the voice changed.

### 2.9 Shorts cuts
See Part 5. Cut **2 to 4 vertical clips** (1080×1920) from your best moments.

## Part 3: The upload, screen by screen

Go to **studio.youtube.com** → **Create** (top right) → **Upload videos** → choose your
file. While it uploads and processes, fill in the 4 screens.

### Screen 1: Details

| Field | Set it to |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Your title from 2.4 |
| **Description** | Your description from 2.5 |
| **Thumbnail** | **Upload file** → your best thumbnail. If you see **Test & compare**, use it and upload all 2–3 versions. YouTube shows each to different viewers and keeps the one with the most **watch time**. |
| **Playlists** | Create a playlist like "Black Holes" or "Space Explained" and add the video. Playlists keep people watching your next video. |
| **Audience** | **No, it's not made for kids**, unless the video is mainly for children (cartoon style, a kids' voice, nursery-style). This is a legal question, so answer honestly. Choosing "Yes" turns off comments, the notification bell and personalised ads. |
| **Age restriction** | **No**, don't restrict |

Now click **Show more** and set:

| Field | Set it to |
|---|---|
| **Paid promotion** | Leave unticked, unless a brand paid you |
| **Altered content** | **Yes** if you used AI to make **realistic-looking** footage that people could mistake for real (e.g. a photoreal AI "telescope view" of something that was never filmed). **No** if it is clearly animation or CGI, or real NASA/ESA imagery. The label doesn't reduce reach. Getting this wrong can get the video taken down. |
| **Automatic chapters** | Leave on (your own timestamps take priority) |
| **Featured places / Automatic concepts** | Leave on |
| **Tags** | Paste from 2.6 |
| **Language and captions certification** | **Video language** = the language spoken. If there's no speech, pick the language of your title. **Caption certification** = **None** |
| **Title and description language** | The language you wrote them in. You need this before adding translations in Part 4. |
| **Recording date and location** | Optional, can be skipped |
| **License** | **Standard YouTube License** |
| **Allow embedding** | **On** (lets blogs and Reddit embed it, which means more views) |
| **Publish to subscriptions feed and notify subscribers** | **On** |
| **Shorts remixing** | **Allow video and audio remixing** (other people's Shorts that remix you link back to you) |
| **Category** | **Science & Technology**. Use **Education** if it's an explainer or **Film & Animation** if it's a cinematic film with no explanation. |
| **Comments** | **On**, with **Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review** |
| **Sort by** | **Top** |
| **Show how many viewers like this video** | **On** |

Click **Next**.

### Screen 2: Video elements

| Element | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Add subtitles** | If there's a voice-over, click **Add** → **Auto-sync**, then paste your script, or **Upload file** if you have an `.srt`. Correct subtitles also let you auto-translate them into other languages later (Part 4.2). |
| **Add an end screen** | **Import from video** if you have one, otherwise **Video + Subscribe** (or **Best for viewer**). It appears in the **last 5–20 seconds**, so your video needs 20 seconds of outro (space visuals, "watch this next"). The video must be at least 25 seconds long. |
| **Add cards** | Add 1–2 cards pointing to your related video or playlist at the moment you mention it |

Click **Next**.

### Screen 3: Checks
YouTube scans for copyright. Wait for **"No issues found."**
If it shows a **copyright claim**, click **See details**. If it says **blocked in some
countries** or **monetized by the owner**, replace or trim that music (Part 2.7) before
you publish. **Don't publish with a claim if you want worldwide reach.**

Click **Next**.

### Screen 4: Visibility
Pick one:
- **Schedule** (recommended): set a time when your audience is online.
  - **India-first audience:** 6:00 to 8:00 PM IST, Friday to Sunday works well.
  - **Worldwide or US audience:** about **9:30 to 11:30 PM IST** (= 11 AM to 1 PM US
    Eastern after US clocks go back on 1 November).
  - After your first few videos, use Studio → **Analytics** → **Audience** → **When your
    viewers are on YouTube** instead of these guesses.
- **Public + "Set as instant Premiere"**: viewers watch together with live chat. Worth it
  once you have some subscribers. On a brand-new channel, just go **Public** or
  **Schedule**.

Click **Schedule** or **Publish**. **Don't move on yet. Do Part 4 now.**

## Part 4: Languages and audio for other countries

YouTube has **no "target countries" setting**. It decides where to push a video based on
who responds to it. What you control is whether people in other countries can
**understand and search** for your video. YouTube shows each viewer the version in their
**app language**, so a viewer in Brazil sees your title in Portuguese and hears the
Portuguese audio automatically. This is the biggest lever for international reach.

All of this is in: Studio → **Content** → click your video → **Languages** (left menu).

### 4.1 Translated title and description: works on every channel, do this first
**Add language** → choose a language → under **Title & description** click **Add** →
paste the translated title and description → **Publish**. Repeat for each language.

Priority languages for space content (biggest YouTube audiences that watch science):

| # | Language | Main countries | Example title: "What Happens If You Fall Into a Black Hole?" |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | English | USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Philippines, worldwide | What Happens If You Fall Into a Black Hole? |
| 2 | Hindi | India | अगर आप ब्लैक होल में गिर जाएं तो क्या होगा? |
| 3 | Spanish | Mexico, Spain, Argentina, Colombia, US Latinos | ¿Qué pasa si caes en un agujero negro? |
| 4 | Portuguese | Brazil, Portugal | O que acontece se você cair em um buraco negro? |
| 5 | Indonesian | Indonesia | Apa yang Terjadi Jika Kamu Jatuh ke Lubang Hitam? |
| 6 | Arabic | Saudi Arabia, Egypt, UAE, Gulf | ماذا يحدث إذا سقطت في ثقب أسود؟ |
| 7 | French | France, Canada, Africa | Que se passe-t-il si vous tombez dans un trou noir ? |
| 8 | German | Germany, Austria, Switzerland | Was passiert, wenn du in ein Schwarzes Loch fällst? |
| 9 | Japanese | Japan | ブラックホールに落ちたらどうなる？ |

Also worth adding for India: Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi.
Translate the **whole description**, including the chapter names (keep the timestamps
unchanged). Leave hashtags in English plus one in the local language.

### 4.2 Subtitles in every language: works on every channel
For a video with a voice-over:
1. Make sure the original-language subtitles are correct (Screen 2 above, or **Languages**
   → your video language → **Subtitles** → **Edit**, since auto-captions often get space
   words wrong: "Schwarzschild", "Betelgeuse", "spaghettification").
2. For each language you added in 4.1 → **Subtitles** → **Add** → **Auto-translate**,
   then **Publish**. For Hindi and other big languages, skim the result.

For a video with **no voice-over** (music only), skip subtitles. Translated titles and
descriptions (4.1) do the job.

### 4.3 Extra audio tracks (multi-language audio): only if your video has a voice-over
This is what "audio for countries" usually means: one video, and the viewer hears it in
their own language.

1. Make a dubbed voice-over for each language. Options: re-record it yourself or with a
   friend, or use an AI dubbing tool (e.g. ElevenLabs Dubbing) that keeps your voice in
   the new language.
2. Export each language as **one full audio file**: **exactly the same length as the
   video**, the **same music and sound effects**, only the voice replaced. WAV or MP3,
   48 kHz.
3. Studio → **Content** → your video → **Languages** → **Add language** → pick the
   language → under **Audio** click **Add** → upload the file → **Publish**.
   If you **don't see an Audio option**, the feature isn't on for your channel yet. Use
   4.1 and 4.2 instead; they still bring in international viewers.
4. Good order to add them: Hindi, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Arabic, French, German.

If your video is music only, **you don't need audio tracks**. Music doesn't need
translating.

### 4.4 Automatic dubbing (if your channel has it)
If you saw **Allow automatic dubbing** in Part 1.4 and it's on, YouTube creates AI dubs of
your voice-over by itself. Check them: **Languages** → each auto-dub → **Preview**.
**Unpublish** any that sound wrong, because a bad dub hurts more than no dub.
It doesn't dub music, and it works best with one clear speaker.

## Part 5: Shorts (the fastest way to new viewers)

Shorts reach people who have never heard of you, then send them to the full video.

1. Cut **2 to 4 clips** from your best moments: **vertical 1080×1920 (9:16)**,
   **15 to 50 seconds** (Shorts can be up to 3 minutes, but shorter loops better).
2. The **first frame must already be the most interesting frame**. No build-up at all.
3. Big on-screen text: a question at the start ("What would you see falling into a black
   hole? 😳"), the answer near the end.
4. Make the end flow back into the start so it **loops**. Loops count as rewatches.
5. **Music:** your own track, YouTube Audio Library, or a sound picked inside the YouTube
   app. **Shorts over 1 minute with a copyright claim get blocked worldwide**, so keep
   those claim-free.
6. Upload the same way (Studio → Create → Upload). A vertical video under 3 minutes
   automatically becomes a Short.
   - Title: short, plus `#shorts #space`.
   - **Related video:** link it to your **full video**. This puts a clickable link on the
     Short.
   - Same audience, language, category and altered-content answers as the full video.
7. Post **one Short a day** for the days after the full video, not all at once.

## Part 6: The first 48 hours

| When | Do this |
|---|---|
| Right after publishing | **Pin a comment** with a question that's easy to answer ("Would you go in? 🕳️ Yes or no?"). Comments signal a video people care about. |
| First hour | Share the link with people who will **actually watch the whole video**: WhatsApp groups, Instagram story, friends interested in space. 10 people who watch to the end are worth more than 100 who leave after 5 seconds. |
| First day | **Reply to every comment.** Heart the good ones. |
| First day | Post in space communities (Reddit, Facebook groups, Discord). **Read each community's self-promotion rules first.** Share it as "I made this", with a still or a short clip, not just a bare link. |
| Day 1–4 | Release your Shorts, one a day (Part 5). |
| If available | Make a **Community post** from the **Posts** tab on your channel, with a striking frame and the link. |

---

## Part 7: Check the numbers after 48 hours, then fix

Studio → **Content** → your video → **Analytics**:

| Number | What it means | If it's low |
|---|---|---|
| **Impressions click-through rate** | % of people who saw the thumbnail and clicked | Below ~3%: **change the thumbnail first, then the title.** You can edit both any time, and the video gets a second chance. |
| **Average view duration / retention graph** | Where people leave | A big drop in the first 30 seconds means the opening is too slow. Fix it in the next video. |
| **Traffic source** | Where viewers come from | "Browse" and "Suggested" growing means YouTube is pushing it |
| **Geography** (Audience tab) | Which countries watch | Add translations (Part 4.1) for any country that shows up and you haven't covered yet |

---

## Part 8: Things that hurt or kill a channel. Never do these.

- Buying views, subscribers or likes, or "sub-for-sub" groups. YouTube removes them and
  can suspend the channel.
- A misleading thumbnail or title (e.g. "REAL footage of a black hole" when it's CGI or AI).
  Viewers leave fast and YouTube stops showing it.
- Deleting and re-uploading the same video to "try again". Change the thumbnail or title
  instead.
- Using other channels' clips or copyrighted songs.
- Changing the title and thumbnail every few hours. Give each version at least
  24–48 hours.
- Marking it "made for kids" just because kids like space, if it isn't actually made for
  kids.

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Phone verified (custom thumbnails unlocked)
- [ ] Exported as MP4 H.264, at 1440p or 4K if possible
- [ ] Opens with the best shot, question within 5 seconds
- [ ] Thumbnail 1280×720, under 2 MB, 2–3 versions
- [ ] Title under 60 characters, curious and true
- [ ] Description: first 2 lines with keywords, chapters from 0:00, credits, 3 hashtags
- [ ] Music claim-free (Audio Library or your own)
- [ ] Audience: not made for kids (unless it is)
- [ ] Altered content answered honestly
- [ ] Category: Science & Technology
- [ ] Video language and title language set
- [ ] End screen + 1–2 cards
- [ ] Checks: no copyright issues
- [ ] Scheduled for peak time
- [ ] Translated titles and descriptions added (at least Hindi, Spanish, Portuguese)
- [ ] Subtitles plus auto-translations (if there's a voice-over)
- [ ] Extra audio tracks (if there's a voice-over and the option is available)
- [ ] 2–4 Shorts cut, linked to the full video, one a day
- [ ] Pinned comment, replies to everyone in the first day
- [ ] Check CTR and retention after 48 hours, change the thumbnail if CTR is under 3%
