# Kanyakumari & Padmanabhaswamy — 30 Aug to 6 Sep 2026

Self-contained itinerary page: `kanyakumari-2026.html`. Open it directly in a
browser — no build step, no dependencies, mobile-first, and it prints cleanly
if you want a paper copy for the train.

**The trip:** Sunday to Sunday, Bengaluru → Kanyakumari → Thiruvananthapuram →
Bengaluru, by overnight train both ways. Two nights at the tip of India, three
in Trivandrum, six full days on the ground.

**Built around three fixed constraints:**

- Padmanabhapuram Palace is closed Mondays — and the arrival day is a Monday,
  so the palace moves to Wednesday, on the drive out of Kanyakumari.
- Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple shuts 12:00–17:00 daily, so darshan gets a
  morning to itself (Thursday).
- Sleeping on the train both ways converts two travel days into two nights.

**Before travelling, re-verify:** train times on IRCTC/NTES (numbers are stable,
timings shift), the darshan slot at booking.sreepadmanabhaswamytemple.org, and
Padmanabhapuram Palace on 04651 250255.
